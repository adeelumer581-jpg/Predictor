# INSTITUTIONAL MARKET KNOWLEDGE MATRIX (DNA-01)

## PHASE 1: CANDLESTICK ARCHITECTURE & PRICE ACTION
### 1. The Psychology of the Print
*   **Marubozu**: Extreme conviction; no shadows. Bullish Marubozu (Open=Low, Close=High) indicates total buyer control.
*   **Doji Clusters**: Market indecision. Standard, Dragonfly (Bullish reversal at bottom), Gravestone (Bearish reversal at top).
*   **Hammers & Hanging Men**: Hammer (at bottom) indicates successful liquidity sweep and rejection of lower prices. Hanging Man (at top) indicates failed support.

### 2. High-Probability Clusters
*   **Engulfing Patterns**: Complete reversal of previous candle's range.
*   **Morning/Evening Stars**: 3-candle transition structures indicating trend exhausted.
*   **Three Soldiers/Crows**: Pure trend momentum confirmation.

## PHASE 2: CHART ARCHITECTURE & FRACTAL GEOMETRY
### 1. Structural Mastery
*   **Head & Shoulders**: The ultimate reversal structure. Confirmation requires neck-line breach with volume.
*   **Cup & Handle**: Long-term accumulation followed by a "shakeout" (the handle).
*   **Fractal Recognition**: Patterns on the 1m chart often mirror structures on the 1D chart (Self-Similarity).

### 2. Breakout Confirmation (The 2.0x Rule)
*   **Volume Expansion**: No breakout is valid unless Volume > 2.0x the 20-period Simple Moving Average of Volume.
*   **Price Deviation**: Confirmation requires a close outside the structural boundary.

## PHASE 3: QUANTITATIVE DATA SPECTRUM
### 1. Algorithmic Indicators
*   **EMA Stack**: 9 (Entry), 21 (Trend), 50 (Support), 200 (Institutional bias).
*   **VWAP**: The true average price paid by all traders. Price < VWAP is Bearish; Price > VWAP is Bullish.
*   **Volume Profile**: 
    *   **HVN (High Volume Node)**: Areas of high interest; act as "Sticky" support/resistance.
    *   **LVN (Low Volume Node)**: Areas of no interest; price travels through these gaps at high velocity.

## PHASE 5: SCALPING & DAY TRADING (HIGH-FREQUENCY)
### 1. The 1% Rule
*   Never risk > 1% of total account equity on a single trade.
*   Position Size = (Total Equity * 0.01) / (Stop Loss Distance).

### 2. 2:1 Reward-to-Risk (The Professional Minimum)
*   Every trade must have a target twice as large as the stop loss.
*   Exit automation: Bracket Orders (Take-Profit & Stop-Loss) must be set instantly.

### 3. Opening Range Breakout (ORB)
*   Wait for the 9:30 - 9:35 AM range to settle.
*   Entry: High-volume break of the 5-minute High (Bullish) or Low (Bearish).
*   Exit: Momentum-based (1-3 minutes) or stop-loss trigger.
